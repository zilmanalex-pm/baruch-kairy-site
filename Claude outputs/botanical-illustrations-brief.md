# Botanical Illustration Brief — Baruch Kairy Professional Website

## Style Reference

Minimalist botanical illustrations in the style of the attached reference image: flat vector art with organic plant forms (leaves, branches, ferns), soft geometric shapes (circles, rings/donuts), and small dot-cluster accents. No shadows, no gradients within shapes — just flat fills with varying opacity. Clean thin lines for leaf veins. The overall feel is calm, professional, and warm — like a high-end wellness or psychology practice.

## Color Palette (strict — use only these)

| Color | Hex | Role |
|-------|-----|------|
| Cerulean teal | `#40798C` | Primary — use for main botanical elements (leaves, branches, stems) |
| Tropical teal | `#70A9A1` | Secondary — leaf vein lines, lighter botanical accents |
| Tea green | `#CFE0C3` | Large soft background circles, light fills |
| Soft green | `#9EC1A3` | Medium accent circles, dot clusters |
| Warm orange | `#FF8200` | Warm accent — small solid circles, ring shapes, select dot clusters. Use sparingly (15-20% of composition) to add warmth without dominating |
| Muted orange | `#FF8200` at 60% opacity | Softer orange for larger shapes when needed |

**Do NOT use:** black, grey, brown, or any colors outside this palette. The background of each illustration should be transparent (PNG with alpha) or match `#EFF5E9` (the site's page background).

## Format & Technical Specs

- **Format:** SVG (preferred) or PNG with transparent background
- **Dimensions:** 800×800px square canvas (will be scaled down to ~400-550px on the site)
- **Style:** Flat vector, no raster textures, no shadows, no 3D effects
- **Composition:** Each illustration should have one main botanical element, 2-3 geometric accent shapes, and 1-2 dot clusters. Leave breathing room — don't fill the entire canvas
- **Opacity variation:** Use opacity (0.3–0.8) to create depth and layering. Background circles should be the most transparent (0.25–0.35), botanical elements medium (0.5–0.7), small accents can be stronger (0.4–0.6)

## Composition Guidelines

- **Asymmetric balance:** Don't center the main element. Offset it like the reference — the leaf occupies about 60% of the canvas, shifted to one side.
- **Layering:** Background circle sits behind the botanical element. Small accents (dots, circles) are scattered in the remaining space.
- **Orange placement:** The orange accent should appear in a different position in each illustration (sometimes top-right, sometimes bottom-left, etc.) so they feel varied when placed across different pages.
- **Leaf style:** Elongated leaf shapes with a single thin vein line down the center of each leaf (lighter teal or green). Leaves attach to a curved main stem.
- **Dot clusters:** Groups of 5-8 small circles (radius 4-6px at 800px canvas) arranged in a loose organic cluster, not a rigid grid.
- **Ring shapes:** A circle with a thick stroke (no fill), creating a donut/ring effect. Stroke width approximately 30-40% of the radius.

## What to Avoid

- Realistic botanical illustration — keep it stylized and minimal
- Harsh edges or sharp geometric patterns
- Text or labels
- Heavy fills that make the illustration feel dense
- More than one main botanical element per illustration
- Pure white elements (the site background is off-white/green-tinted)
